Goals

Grow my Skills

One of my main goals is to continually improve my capabilities as a developer. I always try to practice and maintain my coding skills so that I can keep on learning and getting better. I want to become more proficient in many languages and keep improving the quality of my code.

Grow in my Career

As a co-op student, I want to continue to gain hands-on experience through my co-op placements and build a strong network in the tech industry. I want to explore different areas of the field and contribute to different projects, so I can broaden my understanding of the field.

Make the World a Better Place

The most important goal of mine is to use my skills and knowledge for good, and create a positive impact on the world. Whether it be by working on projects that can help support services that provide aid to those in need, or help improve access to information, or just by helping to make life easier for the average person, I want to perform work that has a tangible benefit to society.
